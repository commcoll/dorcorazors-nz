What does Dorco mean?

The name 'DORCO' combines three elements:

DO

'DO' from Dongyang

The first two letters of Dongyang (meaning "Orient") Light Metal Industries — our original company.

R

'R' from Razor

'R' from 'razor' — representing the core of what we do and have done for over six decades.

CO

'CO' from Company

DORCO was registered as a brand name in 1961 and became our company name in 1977.
