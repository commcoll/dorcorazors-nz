Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DORCO razors, ordering, and delivery.

Products

Yes! All Dorco razor handles and cartridges use our common docking system. This means any Dorco refill cartridge will fit any Dorco razor handle, giving you complete flexibility to try different blade options.
The PACE 6 PRO features six precision blades plus a safety trimmer on the back for detail work. The six-blade design provides an exceptionally close and smooth shave.
A razor system includes both the handle and one or more refill cartridges — everything you need to start shaving. Refill cartridges are replacement blade heads that fit onto your existing handle. Once you have a handle, you only need to buy refill cartridges going forward.
Cartridge life varies depending on use and hair type, but most users find cartridges last between 5–10 shaves. Rinsing your razor thoroughly after each use and allowing it to air dry will help extend cartridge life.
Our lubricating strip contains water-soluble lubricants with natural extracts including chamomile and allantoin. These cool and soothe your skin after shaving, reducing friction between the blades and your skin.
