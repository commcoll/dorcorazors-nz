Our blades are different

Our unique bent blade design and open-flow cartridge system deliver precision, comfort, and easy rinsing — advantages that come from decades of investment in research and development.

Bent Blade Design

Our unique bent blade design allows accurate shaving with precision. This construction allows for an open-flow cartridge with a large passage for easier rinsing — vs. welded blades with the same span.

Micro Span

Thanks to our blades which are formed into angle, we can position them very close together. The narrower the spaces between blades, the less the skin penetrates through them — so you enjoy a safe, smooth shave with less skin irritation.

Common Docking System

If you want to try different blades in our range, you can change your refill cartridges whenever you want. Our common docking system means any of our blade cartridges will fit your razor handle.

Lubricating Strip

Water-soluble lubricants reduce friction between the blades and your skin. Containing natural extracts such as chamomile and allantoin, they cool and soothe your skin after your shave.

Rubber Guard

The soft rubber guard glides smoothly over your skin, preparing it for a comfortable shave and minimising skin irritation throughout your shaving stroke.

Pivoting Head

Responds to the contours of your skin, ensuring a smooth, comfortable shave. The head connects the blade cartridge to the handle, designed to make your razor easy to hold and prevent slipping.

PACE Blade Grinding & Coating

Our grinding process creates long-lasting, precision blades. Then we apply a high-strength thin film coating for an even longer life and better performance.

Open-Flow Blade Cartridge

With our open-backed cartridge design, rinsing your razor after shaving is quick and easy. Thorough rinsing helps protect your blades from corrosion, making them last longer.
